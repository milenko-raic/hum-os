02 · Plan / Authorize

Your active habit charters.

Each habit gets its own rules, starting point and baseline. STOP, REDUCE, BOUND or REPLACE — the product adapts to the goal instead of forcing every user into one model.

Why a charter?

Make the decision before the difficult moment arrives.

A charter is simply a compact plan: what behavior you are changing, what success means, which triggers matter, and what you do instead. The complex runtime stays behind the interface.

03 · Patterns / Structure

See what keeps the habit alive.

HUM-OS turns scattered moments into a behavioral map. A streak tells you how long. A pattern tells you why — and that is where prevention becomes practical.

38%
Routine cue

Repeated context: same time, same place, same automatic sequence.

27%
Stress release

The habit appears as a fast escape from pressure, tension or decision fatigue.

18%
Environment

People, rooms, routes, devices or objects reactivate an old behavior script.

11%
Social cue

Other people or shared rituals make the old behavior feel normal again.

Relationship view

Trigger → automatic script → behavior → short reward → reinforced dependency. HUM-OS interrupts this chain at the earliest practical point. The product does not need to shame the user; it needs to make the relationship legible.

05 · Support / N.O.I.S.E. Harbour

Bring in support without losing control.

The Harbour is secondary by design. It connects the user to useful resources when needed, while the core runtime stays clear and personal.

Port 01

Calm

A five-minute delay and breathing surface for acute cravings, impulses or anxiety-heavy moments.

Port 02

Signal

Prepare a short support message the user can copy manually into their preferred messaging app.

Port 03

Move

Break a location-bound pattern with a short walk, room change or physical context reset.

Port 04

Learn

Explain why a trigger works and how repetition turns a context into an automatic behavioral script.

Port 05

Expert

Some behaviors may require professional guidance, especially where withdrawal, medication or acute health risk is involved.

Port 06

AI Mirror

Summarize patterns and reflect evidence back to the user — advisory only, never an authority over the person.
